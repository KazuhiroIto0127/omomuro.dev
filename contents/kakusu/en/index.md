## Find faces automatically, then hide them

Kakusu detects faces in photos and videos and hides them with mosaic, blur, solid fills, or stickers. In videos, masks follow moving faces. Review the result and save a new copy on your iPhone or iPad. Your originals stay unchanged.

![Video editor with faces automatically masked and a range for each face](/images/apps/screens/kakusu/en/video.jpg 'phone') ![Photo editor listing detected faces, each with its own masking style](/images/apps/screens/kakusu/en/photo.jpg 'phone')

## Choose a look for each face

Turn masking on or off for individual faces. Adjust coverage while watching the preview, from the face alone to hair and neck.

![Face settings with a coverage slider and mosaic, blur, fill and sticker options](/images/apps/screens/kakusu/en/size.jpg 'phone')

## Keep people, hide the background

Open Background in the editor to apply mosaic, blur, or a solid fill. Hide faces and the background at the same time. Background masking applies to the entire photo or the full video.

## Draw a mask where you need it

Add a manual mask and adjust its position and size. In videos, set its start and end times or follow a moving subject with tracking. Tap a range's bar to adjust its timing right on the timeline.

## Review before saving

- Press and hold to compare with the original
- Adjust playback speed to review videos
- Undo edits and resume your work
- Export videos up to 1080p, with or without audio
- No watermarks

## A side panel on iPad and iPhone Duo

On iPad and an unfolded iPhone Duo, the face, background or range you select is edited in a panel on the right, so you can keep watching the video while you edit.

## On-device processing

Photos and videos are not uploaded to a server for face detection, person segmentation, or editing. No account is required.

![Kakusu's start screen, with a button to choose photos and videos](/images/apps/screens/kakusu/en/duo-home.jpg 'duo')

## Before you share

Automatic detection and tracking can miss faces or lose their position, and person/background boundaries may be inaccurate. Review the entire photo or video and add manual masks as needed. Text, license plates, and voices are not hidden automatically.

Longer videos need more processing time and available storage. Keep the app open while saving videos with background masking.
