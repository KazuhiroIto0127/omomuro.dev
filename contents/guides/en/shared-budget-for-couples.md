Keeping a budget as a couple often stalls on two questions: how much should we track together, and who records what? This guide covers a simple way to share a budget without connecting a bank account or credit card, with step-by-step instructions for Kakei Pocket, an iPhone and iPad app.

## Three things to decide first

### 1. Separate shared and personal spending

If every expense lands in one pot, each of you can see exactly where your pocket money goes, and that makes the habit harder to keep. Put **living costs** such as rent, groceries and household goods in a shared bucket, and keep hobbies and personal purchases **separate**.

### 2. Start the month on payday

If your budget month always starts on the 1st but you are paid on the 25th, spending looks bunched up at the end of the month. Starting the month on payday makes "how much is left this month" match how it actually feels.

### 3. Record fixed costs with one tap

Rent and subscriptions cost about the same every month. Register them once and record them with a single tap when they are paid.

## A budget without bank linking

Apps that import transactions automatically are convenient, but they need access to your accounts or cards. If you would rather not share that, a budget you fill in yourself, by hand or from receipts, is a good fit.

Manual entry sounds tedious, but picking an amount and a category takes seconds. Scanning a receipt fills in the date, amount and items on a big shopping day.

## Sharing a budget in Kakei Pocket

Kakei Pocket lets you share a ledger with one partner through iCloud. Sharing is free.

1. Both of you sign in to iCloud on your iPhone or iPad
2. The ledger owner invites their partner from the ledger sharing option in Settings
3. The partner opens the invitation link to join (if it doesn't open from a messaging app, open it again in Safari)

When the partner joins, the ledger on their device is replaced by the shared one. If they want to keep their existing entries, export them to CSV or JSON first.

![Kakei Pocket home screen with this month's balance and spending split into Shared and each partner](/images/apps/screens/kakei-pocket/en/1.jpg 'phone') ![Entry screen: choose a category and Shared or a partner, then type the amount](/images/apps/screens/kakei-pocket/en/2.jpg 'phone')

Each entry is marked Shared, Me or Partner, so household costs and personal spending live in the same ledger without getting mixed up. The month can start on any day from the 1st to the 28th to match your payday.

## Small habits that help

- **Record on the spot**: enter the amount as you leave the shop; notes and item details are optional
- **Compare with last month**: seeing which categories went up or down is a good starting point for a chat
- **Don't aim for perfect**: a few missing small purchases don't matter; reviewing together once a month does
- **Bring your old records**: a budget you kept in a spreadsheet can be imported from a CSV file

## Common questions

### Do we need a paid plan to share?

No. Sharing works in the free version. If you want to remove ads and the receipt scan limit, the owner buys Pro once and it applies to the partner too.

### Can I share with a partner on Android?

No. Kakei Pocket is for iPhone and iPad only. Note that amounts are in Japanese yen and receipt scanning is designed for Japanese receipts.
