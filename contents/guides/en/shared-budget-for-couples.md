If you keep a budget as a couple, sharing one ledger between you is what makes it stick.

This guide walks through sharing a budget in the free Kakei Pocket app, with real screenshots. No bank account or credit card is connected.

## What you need

- An iPhone or iPad for each of you (iOS / iPadOS 26 or later)
- The [Kakei Pocket](/works/kakei-pocket/en) app (free, sharing included)
- Both of you signed in to iCloud

## Steps

### 1. Invite your partner

Open Sharing in Settings and tap “Invite partner”. Send the link it creates through Messages or LINE.

![The Sharing screen: three steps for inviting, with an “Invite partner” button below](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/6-sharing.jpg 'phone')

Your partner installs Kakei Pocket and then opens the link. If it doesn't open inside a messaging app, open it again in Safari.

Each link works once. If it doesn't arrive, create a new one.

> When your partner joins, the ledger on their device is replaced by the shared one. If they want to keep their entries, export them to CSV first.

### 2. Record each entry as Shared or personal

Tap + at the bottom right, choose an amount and a category, then tap “Add entry”.

![The entry screen: below the categories, a switch between Shared and each partner, with a number pad below](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/2-add.jpg 'phone')

Next to the date you can switch between Shared and each of you. Put rent and groceries under **Shared** and hobbies or personal shopping under **your own name**, so living costs and pocket money don't get mixed up.

### 3. Snap your receipts

On a big shopping day, photograph the receipt or pick it from Photos. The date, amount and items are filled in, so you just check and save.

![An entry filled in from a receipt: ¥500 with the item “お菓子” (snacks)](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/3-receipt.jpg 'phone')

Text is read on your device. Receipt scanning is designed for Japanese receipts.

### 4. Record fixed costs with one tap

Add rent and subscriptions under Recurring payments. When one is paid, tap its record button for that month.

![Recurring payments: a gym, cloud storage, video streaming and more, each with a button to record this month](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/4-subs.jpg 'phone')

Nothing is recorded automatically, so months you didn't pay never fill up with entries.

### 5. See both of you on Home

Home shows this month's balance plus Shared and each partner's spending.

![Home: this month's balance, then the Shared and per-partner totals and this week's entries](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/1-home.jpg 'phone')

In Settings, the month can start on any day from the 1st to the 28th. Matching it to payday makes “how much is left this month” easier to read.

### 6. Compare with last month

Report compares your spending with the same day last month, and shows which categories went up or down.

![Report: this month's and last month's spending pace as a line chart, with a breakdown by category below](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/7-charts.jpg 'phone')

Looking at this screen together once a month is enough.

## Moving from another budget

A budget you kept in a spreadsheet can be imported from a CSV file in Settings. You can check the number of entries and the date range before importing.

![The CSV import review: 11 entries to import, the date range, and the expense and income totals](/images/guides/shared-budget-for-couples/en/8-import.jpg 'phone')

## Pricing

Recording, fixed costs, export and sharing are all free. The free version shows ads and allows 10 receipt scans a month per device.

When the ledger owner buys Pro once, both of you get no ads and unlimited receipt scans. There is no monthly fee.

Kakei Pocket is for iPhone and iPad only, and all amounts are in Japanese yen.
